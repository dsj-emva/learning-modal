# Business brief for the context agent (written by a human, edit per customer)

## What we sell
EMVA: a service that scores inbound B2B leads and sends the score back to Google Ads and Meta
as a conversion value, so the ad platforms bid for leads that turn into revenue, not just form fills.

## Who buys it
- B2B companies that spend real money on paid ads (Google, Meta, LinkedIn) and generate inbound leads.
- They have a sales team and a CRM, and the pain is "we get leads but can't tell which ads bring buyers".
- Typical buyer: marketing or revenue leadership (Head of Growth, CMO, RevOps, Founder at smaller firms).

## Good signs a human salesperson looks for
- A concrete problem in their own words (tracking, attribution, lead quality, CRM to ads).
- Specifics: budget, ad spend, team size, a deadline, a tool they are replacing.
- A real company that plausibly advertises and has a sales process.

## Bad signs
- Students, researchers, job seekers, vendors pitching us, competitors.
- No company, or a company that clearly doesn't advertise or sell B2B.
- Text that is filler, pasted marketing copy, or unrelated to our product.

## Deal-breakers
- Not a business, or no paid advertising at all.
